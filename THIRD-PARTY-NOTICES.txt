- The model weights in this repository (`model.safetensors`) are licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (`LICENSE_MODEL`); commercial use requires separate permission. The inference code is on [GitHub muscgab/manga-ocr-CPT](https://github.com/muscgab/manga-ocr-CPT) under Apache License 2.0.
- The weights are initialised from [kha-white/manga-ocr-base](https://huggingface.co/kha-white/manga-ocr-base) (mangaOCR), whose model card declares Apache License 2.0 (full text as in `LICENSE`) and lists Manga109-s as training data. According to its configuration, the mangaOCR encoder comes from facebook/deit-base-patch16-224 and the decoder from cl-tohoku/bert-base-japanese-char-v2. The inference code is a re-implementation and does not depend on the manga-ocr package or `transformers`.
- Real samples for the continued pre-training derive from [JustANormalTinkerer/animetext-ocr](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JustANormalTinkerer/animetext-ocr) (revision `9c70c6b`), declared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- The images of that dataset come from AnimeText ([arXiv 2510.07951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07951)). The real kana glyphs used by the synthetic handwriting were also cut from AnimeText images and upscaled 4× with [Real-ESRGAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10833) (RealESRGAN_x4plus_anime_6B); this repository does not contain those glyphs.
- Synthetic training data was generated by the project renderer. This repository contains no training images, font files or AnimeText-OCR images.
- No image from the JMangaBench_Read candidate pool or benchmark was used for training.
- Acknowledgements: [mangaOCR](https://github.com/kha-white/manga-ocr) (architecture and initial weights), AnimeText and AnimeText-OCR (real training crops), Real-ESRGAN (upscaling of the kana glyphs).
