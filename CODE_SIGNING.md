## Privacy policy

See the [OverTranslate Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

OverTranslate processes OCR locally. When the user chooses an online
translation service, recognized text may be sent to the selected third-party
translation provider. Diagnostic information is uploaded only when explicitly
requested by the user.
